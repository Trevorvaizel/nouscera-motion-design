# Speech to Sign | What should become visible, and when?

## 0. Why this audit exists

The author confirmed that the film should **not** force a hard choice between abstract imagery and complete immersive scenes. A detailed, literal object can appear isolated in darkness. A small cluster of actions can be reconstructed without a full environment. A different scene can arrive by cut, black reset or sound. **Continuity of meaning matters; continuity of physical scenery is conditional.**

The prior Rhythm A animatic is 49.25 seconds with one temporary voice. This audit preserves its **actual spoken text** and examines the image's semiotic work at the **speech-event level** before revising.

**The source has priority:** _The Human at the Door_, “The fog,” pp. 7–11. Page 7 introduces multiple conflicting but plausible instructions, p. 8 explains why even true advice can arrive before its place is known, p. 9 establishes the human circumstances in which advice lands, p. 10 distinguishes placement from reflexive advice rejection, and p. 11 closes on “Not certainty. Placement.”

**Not a new artifact family:** This is an A03/A04 annex feeding selected A05 shot contracts and A06 tests. It does **not** override the approved A01 meaning.

## 1. Audit questions for every spoken unit

1. **Heard content:** Exact provisional spoken words and whether they are source paraphrase, interpretation, or explicit source text.
2. **Intended inference:** What should become newly conceivable or distinguishable to the listener?
3. **Sign vehicle:** Is an isolated *concrete object*, action, trace, partial scene, environment, symbol, relational graphic or intentional absence appropriate?
4. **Depiction scope:** How much context must appear? **Object detail and scene completeness are independent.**
5. **Audio-visual timing:** Should the image anticipate, coincide with, follow, withhold, interrupt or counterpoint the phrase?
6. **Continuity:** Is a physical morph necessary, or can an edit/black reset preserve the meaning better?
7. **Interpretant/leakage:** What unintended story might viewers construct?

Selection is always **phrase-specific and testable**; no universal rule that every noun needs an illustration.

### Important source omission in the existing narration

The first rough voice does **not explicitly carry the contextual pressures** described on p. 9: money, hope, family, access, time and unequal ability to absorb risk. This is a genuine weakness under approved TR-002; A visual of a generic thinking man cannot silently represent all of that.

**Narration candidate for revision, not approved:** “And these answers don't arrive in an empty life. They arrive inside time, money, and consequences.” This is a **new paraphrase inspired by p. 9**, not a source quote. If used, it requires a corresponding short audio-visual event between S08 and S09 or around the contradiction. A single object such as an unpaid bill must not be used to assert an invented specific biography. Alternative: convey pressure through choices, voice and an explicitly situated personal context without biography.

## 3. What we stop doing

- **Stop equating black/sparse with abstract iconography.** A literal, expressive, highly detailed chair or hand can occupy a black field.
- **Stop equating detail with a full environment.** An object can have texture, depth and emotional character without a world behind it.
- **Stop using identical boxes for semantically different instructions** unless the sameness has a specific narrative function.
- **Stop making advice automatically flow into a single good contact** through a deterministic arrow.
- **Stop treating every visual relationship as a physical morph.** A cut, separate miniature scene, silence or black reset can be more precise.
- **Stop expecting a continuous AI video from isolated designed images.** Local AI motion is used where it conveys a needed relation.
- **Stop selecting images by noun matching alone.** We must evaluate what the viewer is likely to infer.

## 5. Concrete next executable edit

**Animatic v0.2 should first replace three critical misleading simplifications**, without trying to build the whole film at final quality:

1. **Opening (S01–S02):** Replace generic question text emphasis with a more grounded, concrete human fragment against black. Keep a pause.
2. **Advice event (S04–S06):** Experiment with isolated *specific* engraved objects rather than three identical boxes, differentiating the referents of three plausible instructions.
3. **Placement (S10–S12):** Remove the deterministic advice→contact arrow and use an unresolved, contingent relationship or delayed disclosure.

Then insert or otherwise communicate the source's **human pressures**, and keep EX-001 as a local, short immersive placeholder while its morph physics are independently repaired.

**Do not preselect all imagery from this memo.** Author should review the corrected correspondence logic; next produce a sparse *micro animatic* of **S04–S06 plus S10–S12** or another bounded segment to test whether the changed sign vehicles improve interpretation. A full remake of all 49 seconds is unnecessary before this.
