## 6 | Derivation and force transparency

For **every new consequential asset or shot**, its local card or A06 receipt must expose the full **derivation chain**:

\`SRC page/phrase -> approved A01 claim -> A03 viewer inference -> relevant force/question -> contrasted mechanisms -> selected sign/action/interval -> anchor IDs -> generation/model settings -> observed output -> author or cold-viewer response -> decision/revision\`.

### Example: S09 pressure

- **SRC:** p.9 “Just start” in different financial circumstances.
- **Meaning protection:** D-014, true advice can be unplaced; don't invent biography.
- **Observed failure:** D-026, narrated pressure not felt by the author.
- **Scalpels:** **P07-F01** what action changed, **P04-F03** what grabs attention, **P01-F02/F03** what biographical trait leaks into interpretation.
- **Candidate mechanism:** one illustrated threshold where a reach toward action meets an existing obligation; gesture **actually hesitates**.
- **Critical veto:** “The person is definitively in debt” / “waiting is always correct” / “moving camera equals movement.”
- **A06:** actual observed gesture, line consistency, viewer-reported consequence and limits. No such accepted motion proof yet.

### Example: S11 recognition

- **SRC:** p.10 question about the decision or contact that advice would help.
- **Meaning protection:** D-014 and the p.11 open future.
- **Observed failure:** D-026, smaller-question ending did not land.
- **Scalpels:** **P01-F04/P03-F01** image's explanatory contribution; **P07-F01** changed human action; **P02-F06** avoid unwarranted solution.
- **Candidate:** human stops accumulating advice, returns to an existing question, **visibly begins reframing it**; if lettering is generated inaccurately, render text separately in compositing.
- **Critical veto:** single selected “right” path or successful reply appears.
- **A06:** would viewers say the person changed the **question**, not obtained the answer?

## 8 | Standing update rules: this stays a useful living artifact

After **each brick**, the studio must:

1. Update **the one-line CURRENT/NEXT fields** in this map and [STATUS](../../STATUS.md). **Do not leave two conflicting next steps.**
2. Update the relevant **shot/asset status** (queued -> candidate -> tested -> accepted-for-next-stage; avoid “approved” unless author actually selects).
3. Append the **brick ID**, artifact link, exact learned result and known failures to the history. Never erase failed experiments.
4. Add/update the **decision ID** only for a meaningful authorial or methodological choice. Research hypotheses are not automatically decisions.
5. Log precise **source page(s), stage, force code/question, image/voice/motion conditions and evidence class** for the specific new asset.
6. Promote methodology confidence only after prospective prediction + observation + transfer test, not an attractive take.
7. Maintain **both** a primary integrated creative deliverable and traceable process receipts. The film must move forward, not become documentation alone.

### Stop/reorient signals

- We are making arbitrary isolated images without reference to shot, source or viewer inference.
- We are adding fidelity only because the current image is plain, without stating which layer failed.
- We ask the author to make routine AI tool/shot-order decisions that the studio can derive.
- We call a rough, a still or a six-frame GIF a successful human-motion proof.
- We let an experiment silently revise the source's qualified claim.

When any signal appears, return to **Section 0**, read the most recent decision/shot receipt and continue from the current brick rather than start a new conceptual branch.
