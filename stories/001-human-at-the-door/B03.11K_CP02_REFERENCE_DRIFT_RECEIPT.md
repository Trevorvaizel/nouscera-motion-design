## Exact learning boundary

- **Observed:** Two attempted AI generations did **not** preserve the same character, pose, notebook and camera or the intended 9:16 aspect ratio. The output can appear impressively engraved yet fail as a reusable continuity asset.
- **Not established:** That this AI provider can *never* preserve a reference, or that AI-first illustrated filmmaking is impossible. The tool's effective reference attachment/editing path was **not independently verified**.
- **Rejected workaround:** Calling the landscape output “CP-02 approved”, merely cropping to 9:16 or saying “same style” when the person and prop geometry changed.
- **Related case:** EX-001 showed a similar **difference between visual similarity and identity-bearing transformation**. This is a new **visual continuity** case rather than a successful image-to-video attempt.
