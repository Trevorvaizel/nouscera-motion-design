## How these findings change our next attempt

**Do not call the GIF a successful proof.** The test distinguishes *background change* from *semantic metamorphosis*: keeping the subject stable is not enough if the relationship that should change is not visibly authored.

The smallest next improvement is **not another full story**. It is a native portrait pair with the desk/table contour mapped between states, with the same human and an unoccupied opposite place, followed by a motion trial focused on that contour.
