# Film 001 | Continuity Pack v0.1 | Exact Asset Specification

> **Companion:** [Master Experiment Map](MASTER_EXPERIMENT_MAP_v01.md). **Purpose:** produce a small, reusable **visual identity** that supports consistent future shot anchors, rather than six independent attractive pictures. This document specifies the next six AI-generated reference stills and their derivation trails. **No images are marked complete yet.**

## 0 | Why this pack exists, and what it is NOT

The selected 13-shot film has recurring human, notebook and room elements, plus the **two hypothetical conditions** the author recognized in Design B and the **human-action/recognition** moments they reported missing.

A motion model cannot reliably preserve the same subject across S08, S09 and S11 if our input illustrations depict **different people, camera worlds and object geometries**. Conversely, locking every shot into one identical room would betray the user's approval of **independent scenes, isolated objects and black voids**. The desired continuity is **meaning, human identity, selected props and engraved visual physics**, with **spatial continuity only when an actual shot needs it**.

**This pack is not a final film storyboard, character biography, six finished shots, or proof of animated action.** Master stills can supply keyframes and style references. Further start/intermediate/end *action anchors* will be derived from them for specific shots.

## 1 | Shared visual constitution, before individual prompts

**These are proposed constraints from the A04 Bible and D-018/D-021; the actual images may reveal exceptions worth recording.**

### 1.1 The medium

- **Portrait 9:16**, with a working framing target around **1080 x 1920** when the chosen image tool supports it. Record actual generated dimensions honestly. Do not stretch imagery to simulate native ratio.
- **Monochrome** black/white scratchboard, etched engraving, fine contour/hatching/stipple, controlled soft grayscale *only for line anti-alias and rendered volume*, not colorful cinematic photographic lighting.
- Black is **active unformed space**, not simply dark paint on a fully described room. A detailed person, hand or object may appear alone in black; not all visual events need a full environment.
- Rich linework where it helps recognize **a hand, an obligation or the person**, less detail where it would compete with the action.
- **2.5D implied spatial volume**, plausible table/hand perspective, clear near/mid/far depth when an enacted moment requires it. No mandatory editable 3D meshes.

### 1.2 Continuity invariants

- **One recurring human for this film**, with a recognizable silhouette, hand proportions, posture family and neutral practical clothing. **Appearance is a studio candidate, not a factual specification from the essay.** Do not encode a concrete personal hardship, employer, ethnicity, age or financial history as source fact.
- **One recurring notebook**: stable approximate shape, binding, page orientation, margin, relationship to person's hand and work surface. Legible exact words should be **designed in compositing**, not entrusted to generated handwriting.
- **One base work surface** with consistent visible edge geometry and tabletop proportion. It can be reused in intended continuous scenes; it may deliberately disappear in independent vignettes.
- **One room grammar**: quiet inhabited scale, selective practical details, controlled table/lamp/window or other landmarks after the initial image is selected. Do not preemptively lock details absent from the source.
- **Same engraved mark vocabulary** across all six, even if background fullness and camera viewpoint vary.
- **The same advice in two contexts must stay the same**: CP-03 and CP-04 are matched **hypothetical conditions**, not two biographical life histories. Their differences must be **relevant to consequence**, not “rich person vs poor person” stereotypes.
- **No final product/sale or performed successful customer contact**, no AI villain, no predetermined golden path.

### 1.3 Proposed camera family

- **CP-01:** restrained three-quarter medium framing; hand and notebook readable; close enough for human stakes.
- **CP-02:** wider near-matching 3/4 view to establish what surrounds CP-01.
- **CP-03 and CP-04:** **matched view and same action threshold**, where the relevant background/context differences are readable. Preserve subject size/pose/major props so context changes, not identity or camera.
- **CP-05:** deliberate closer crop of the same action threshold; hand, resources and competing obligation share an intelligible frame. Favor **gesture legibility** over ornamental engraving.
- **CP-06:** notebook/hand close view related to CP-01/02; enough spatial cue to know it belongs to the same human.

**Camera matching is strict where the two frames are a comparison or anchor of one actual action.** It is not a mandate for identical cameras in all 13 eventual shots.

## 2 | Exact candidate cards and derivation trail

### CP-01 | The human who has a question

**Narrative job:** Establish an actual person carrying an unresolved beginning; they are neither lazy nor already certain.

**Source:** p.7, late-night recurring "Where do I start?" and p.9's later explanation of situated pressure.  
**Shot dependencies:** S01, S09, S10, S11.  
**Forces:** **P02-F01** (what story does one visible person imply?), **P04-F01** (what is recognized first?), **P05-F01** (figure-ground integrity), **P13-F02** (beautiful portrait versus story fitness).

**Visual target:** One approachable human adult with practical, unremarkable clothing, posture and hand readable, seated with an **open, not-yet-solved** notebook. Enough hints of inhabitation to suggest a real person, with black negative space still dominant. Avoid tearful despair, revelation glow or an artistically elaborate “genius” archetype.

**Composition rule:** Hand, page and human relationship must survive small-phone viewing. Provide space for later graphic emergence.

**Reject if:** The image already tells a complete biography, centers a “successful founder” stereotype, or is a decorative portrait whose decision/hand isn't perceptible.

### CP-02 | The base room, not an all-purpose universe

**Narrative job:** Establish the environment the human returns to after worlds of advice and information.

**Source:** pp.7-8, evening, advice viewing and increasingly full notebook.  
**Dependencies:** CP-01 for person and notebook identity; S01, S06, S10, optional local S08 reference.  
**Forces:** **P06-F01** (camera and perspective must agree), **P05-F01** (room emerges from black without losing figures), **P04-F03** (workspace landmarks must not dominate the person), **P12-F03** (future consistency).

A practical, believable inhabited workspace that **dissolves toward pure black** at the edges. Not a wealthy influencer studio or a chaotic room implying incompetence.

**Reject if:** Person, notebook, table geometry or figure scale differ inexplicably from CP-01; scene is fully filled with unnecessary furniture; scene automatically implies their personal background.

### CP-03 | Context A, more absorbable cost

**Narrative job:** Visually establish one **hypothetical** condition where a modest experiment is affordable enough to try.

**Source:** p.9, “‘Just start’ lands differently when failure is absorbable.”  
**Shot dependencies:** S08 contrast; image/pose partner to CP-04; later S09 timing.  
**Forces:** **P01-F02/F03** (what circumstance is transferred?), **P02-F03** (this is an alternative, not a first action followed by the second), **P04-F03** (notice the permission for a reversible trial), **P13-F02** (avoid “happy rich person” image).

**Visual target:** The same person, camera size, action threshold and proposed modest materials as CP-04. Context permits a small reversible attempt without immediate competing demand. The hand is **still deciding**, not succeeding. The absence of pressure should not be a triumphant mansion or excess wealth.

**Explicit constant across CP-03/04:** Same candidate instruction, same human, same essential props, comparable viewpoint. This contrast should survive phone-scale inspection.

**Reject if:** The viewer could explain the difference only as “rich and poor”, “happy and sad”, or two unrelated scenes, rather than a changed cost of the **same** potential move.

**Output ID:** \`CP03_context_A_absorbable_trial_candidate01.png\`. **Status:** Queued.

### CP-04 | Context B, higher immediate consequence

**Narrative job:** Make the *same* suggested experiment encounter a second legitimate claim on limited money/time/resources.

**Source:** p.9, the same “Just start” sentence when money may be needed for rent, family time, access, reputation or other costs. These are illustrative situations, not established protagonist biography.  
**Shot dependencies:** S08 contrast partner and S09 pressure.  
**Forces:** **P01-F02/F03** (avoid false life story), **P02-F06** (do not invent concrete obligations as facts), **P04-F03** (attention to competing claim), **P07-F01** (future scene needs actual hesitation).

**Visual target:** Mirror CP-03's person, hand height, work surface, provisional materials, camera and general engraving. Make an **existing competing obligation** perceptible through a deliberate localized change: for example a neutral, unaddressed essential-expense document, a dated obligation or an unfinished necessary task, **without** legible identity/amount/address and without assuming a literal rent crisis. The person has not made the decision.

**Reject if:** Scene implies the person is definitively in debt, that waiting is always correct, or a dramatic victim biography. The contextual difference must be one of **action costs**, not moral worth.

**Output ID:** \`CP04_context_B_competing_obligation_candidate01.png\`. **Status:** Queued.

### CP-05 | The pressure threshold, an action waiting to happen

**Narrative job:** Establish correct staging for a **real gesture to be animated later**: reach toward undertaking -> notice competing claim -> hesitate -> unresolved hold. This still is **a reference**, not proof the gesture occurred.

**Source:** p.9 differentiated action costs; **author feedback** B03.11F: pressure was heard but not felt.  
**Shot dependency:** S09 action start/hold family; inherit CP-01, CP-02 and CP-04 identity.  
**Forces:** **P07-F01** (interframe action must be authored), **P07-F07** (motion earns its presence if the decision's change is the meaning), **P04-F03** (hand's interruption must be the focal event), **P12-F03** (hands/props stable through motion).

**Visual target:** Richly engraved close/medium composition, hand just **before** commitment, provisional making materials/resource, and a distinct legitimate competing claim in the same readable visual space. Preserve uncommitted state; no actual completed choice in this image.

**Motion handoff:** After accepting this *staging reference*, derive separate \`S09_A_START\`, \`S09_B_CONFLICT\` and \`S09_C_HOLD\` images with matched viewpoint and physically traceable hand path. The later AI output must show movement, not just camera drift.

**Reject if:** It merely shows “worried person with money,” has a static spread of unrelated props, duplicates a literal high-stakes financial biography, or its hand pose cannot logically lead into a coherent reach/hesitation.

**Output ID:** \`CP05_pressure_threshold_candidate01.png\`. **Status:** Queued.

### CP-06 | The inquiry returns to the notebook

**Narrative job:** Place the same human with the *same* notebook after advice has accumulated, ready to shift from an unbounded problem to a smaller **answerable inquiry**.

**Source:** pp.8,10-11, filled notebook -> question returns -> “What decision or contact is this answer supposed to help with?”  
**Shot dependencies:** S06/S10/S11, optional S13 residue.  
**Forces:** **P01-F04/P03-F01** (visual must add meaningful change beyond narration), **P07-F01** (pen moves toward changing question in later take), **P02-F06** (do not imply answer already selected), **P09-F04** (pause can be productive).

**Visual target:** Close engraving of recurring hand and notebook. Earlier fragments of advice remain as **suggestive non-readable marks**; one page area is left for later accurately composited, modest inquiry. The hand is at a meaningful **before** position, not already holding a triumphant completed answer.

**Text policy:** **Do not rely on AI to spell exact question text.** During edit, layer precise typography or vector strokes. Any final words are proposed adaptation and require script/source review.

**Motion handoff:** \`S11_A_NOTES\` -> \`S11_B_STOP\` -> \`S11_C_BOUNDED_QUESTION\`. Preserve the notebook page orientation, hand anatomy and spatial relations across generated frames. This is **not** a mandatory AI handwriting test; the recognition can be enacted through attention and page arrangement with exact words added later.

**Reject if:** The selected answer appears already solved, handwritten text is nonsense yet essential, the notebook is not recognizable from CP-01/02, or the image is a generic motivational quote.

**Output ID:** \`CP06_inquiry_notebook_candidate01.png\`. **Status:** Queued.

## 3 | Common reference prompt grammar, not six independent prompts

**Generation principle:** **Generate CP-01 first, then use the accepted CP-01 image as a *visual reference* for CP-02.** Extend the same subject/setting to CP-03 and CP-04 as a matched contrast. Derive CP-05 and CP-06 from accepted identity and geometry. Do not generate six disconnected “beautiful pictures” and pretend they form a continuity family.

### Common image design language

> Vertical cinematic monochrome engraving on a pure black field. Fine white scratchboard lines, restrained etching, stippling and localized cross-hatching create anatomically believable human hands, legible material surfaces and quiet implied depth. White marks emerge from empty black rather than filling every part of the canvas. The graphic hierarchy privileges the human's incomplete action and the question or consequence at stake. No color, no glossy photorealistic skin, no spectacle for its own sake, no fixed astronaut character, no source-unsupported biography. Preserve reference subject, clothing, notebook, table edge and camera geometry wherever the shot contract requires continuity.

**Specific shot information must be added from CP-01..CP-06 cards above.** “No source-unsupported biography” is a semantic rule, not a naive negative-prompt guarantee.

### Reference-control strategy

1. **First master CP-01:** Generate candidate variants only until one can pass identity, gesture and person-question readability; record rejected reasons rather than cherry-pick beauty.
2. **CP-02:** Extend from CP-01, keep subject/notebook/table consistent. Specify what changes: framing and environmental extent, **not** identity.
3. **CP-03/04 as *paired comparative art*:** Use the same source reference and composition. Derive **B from A**, preserving camera/pose/props while introducing a localized contextual competing claim. Do not independently roll two separate demographics/rooms.
4. **CP-05:** Crop/recompose from accepted B context and the same person; shape a later coherent hand trajectory.
5. **CP-06:** Return to accepted room/notebook/hand identities. The existing pressure scene is **not automatically the master actor**.
6. **Contact sheet:** Present all six in one visual inspection grid with fixed labels and version IDs, plus 9:16 phone thumbnails. Look for drift *between assets*, not only defects within one image.

**If the current image tool cannot reliably image-reference prior outputs or enforce matching visual geometry**, record a real capability limitation and revise the asset strategy (image editing, composited layers or different supported generation method). **Do not claim character identity is locked because prompt wording matched.**

## 4 | Acceptance tests for the pack before S08/S09/S11 anchors

### A. Noncompensatory story/meaning test

- [ ] Same recognizable human question persists. No specific false biography has been introduced.
- [ ] CP-03 and CP-04 express *different costs of the same possible instruction*, not different classes of people or automatic action order.
- [ ] CP-05 shows an **uncompleted** choice that a later shot can legitimately animate.
- [ ] CP-06 returns to the real notebook and an unanswered decision/contact question.
- [ ] Nothing implies AI advice is bad, the startup has succeeded, validation occurred or future is settled.

### B. Figure, camera, material and style test

- [ ] Person's silhouette, hand proportions, clothing family and notebook are recognizably coherent in all applicable images.
- [ ] CP-03/04 share composition, subject scale and relevant prop placement closely enough for direct comparison.
- [ ] CP-05 can be derived into consistent physical poses without multiplying fingers or redesigning furniture.
- [ ] CP-06 notebook binding/handedness/page direction agree with the base shot.
- [ ] Pure-black negative-space grammar survives; hatching does not bury the focal relation.
- [ ] All principal objects readable in a small portrait playback preview.
- [ ] Art does not silently change to colored painting, glossy 3D render or generic photographic filter.

### C. Production and provenance test

- [ ] Store original AI output with asset ID, source images/refs, prompt, actual model/tool and settings **as returned**, dimensions, and version.
- [ ] Save SHA-256 or analogous reproducible file identifier and local storage location, with actual path verified.
- [ ] Record why any candidate was selected, rejected or revised. Avoid claiming no errors without inspecting it.
- [ ] Preserve a **contact sheet** and a **one-page A06 continuity receipt**.
- [ ] Note limitations: missing seeds, absent reference-image control, model-generated text instability, image edit drift and any unsupported formats.
- [ ] **Author review boundary:** visually coherent working masters may be **ready for shot anchors**, but actual final art direction approval belongs to author and is logged separately.

**A polished collection of unmatching images fails the pack.** Beauty cannot compensate for missing reference identity or wrong semantic transfer (P13-F02).
