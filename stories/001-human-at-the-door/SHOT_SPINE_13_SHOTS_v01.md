# Film 001 | The Fog | Full story-order shot spine v0.1

## Decision correction

The author asked **which shots make up the original Human at the Door film and how the studio should move sequentially**, after repeated attempts to delegate single-shot selection and physical performance capture to the author.

**Production ownership correction:** The studio owns the complete source-to-shot derivation, shot ordering, anchor designs, AI-first generation attempts, checks, revision plans and integration. The author can review consequential creative tradeoffs, but is **not assigned camera recording, acting, provider operation or deciding which isolated shot should be made next**.

This document is a **complete end-to-end shot spine** proposed by the studio. Approximate 73-second sequence, 13 editorial shots and six *recognition movements*. Times are layout allowances, not approved narration or locked cuts. Exact scenes, character identity, imagery and durations remain testable candidates.

## Non-negotiable source commitments

- **p.7:** A person asks "Where do I start?" and encounters credible advice that can conflict. The first video seems convincing; later counsel offers different inquiries and contradictory timing of building.
- **p.8:** AI supplies a polished plan, advice multiplies and the notebook grows. The problem is not that everything learned is false; true advice may arrive before its place is known.
- **p.9:** A sentence may be true and not yet usable. Advice lands amid time, money, family, class, access, reputation and unequal capacity to absorb risk. Example: "Just start" feels different when failure is absorbable than when money was meant for rent. These are **illustrative comparisons**, not known biography of the film's protagonist.
- **p.10:** More information may soothe temporarily; the old question returns. The first task is neither rejecting advice nor assuming every answer deserves the next move. The smaller inquiry is: **What decision or contact is this answer supposed to help with?**
- **p.11:** "Not certainty. Placement."
- **Director's source protection:** No villainous AI, no mandatory startup recipe, no magical correct path, no depicted confirmed sale/validation, no fixed real founder biography.

## Initial original adaptation voice sketch

**Provisional, studio-written; not the essay's verbatim voiceover or author-approved script.** Silence and sound pauses are part of the final composition. When timing a read, revise the allowance rather than forcing speech unnaturally.

> It begins with a question that won't leave you. Where do I start?
>
> One video says: validate before you build. Another says: ask customers. Another: watch what they actually do.
>
> Make something small. Don't build until someone pays.
>
> Then AI offers a plan. Every answer sounds reasonable. The notebook fills. But the question is still there.
>
> Advice doesn't arrive in an empty life.
>
> "Just start" means one thing when a mistake is affordable. Another when the money already belongs somewhere else. The instruction hasn't changed. The conditions have.
>
> Then the old question returns: what do I actually do next?
>
> Perhaps the useful move isn't another answer. What decision would this advice help with? What real contact could push back?
>
> The future is still open. Not certainty. Placement.

**Narration-source guard:** This sketch compresses the passage and is not a substitute for the author's exact prose. The line *"Perhaps the useful move isn't another answer"* is an interpretive paraphrase and must not imply researching more is universally bad. The phrase *"Not certainty. Placement."* is source wording.

## Shot-to-shot causal semantics

| Transition | Intended relation | Forbid |
|---|---|---|
| S01 -> S02 | Person **encounters** credible advice | Already adopts/executes that advice |
| S02 -> S03 / S04 | Multiple **alternative** suggestions | One mandatory recipe |
| S04 -> S05 | Guidance **accumulates** | Having performed the build step successfully |
| S05 -> S06 | Information grows faster than applicability | Everything shown is false or useless |
| S06 -> S07 | Interruption permits contradiction to be registered | A wipe that destroys all learning |
| S07 -> S08 | Shift attention from **answers** to **conditions of use** | New instruction is declared objectively correct |
| S08 -> S09 | A hypothetical difference in context produces **a felt tradeoff** | One actual protagonist has a claimed biographical debt/rent event |
| S09 -> S10 | Person still faces the original question | Hestitation is always wise or definitive |
| S10 -> S11 | A **new way of asking** becomes possible | Guaranteed answer or universal path |
| S11 -> S12 | Potential feedback/contact becomes thinkable | Already received customer validation or sale |
| S12 -> S13 | A modest, inspectable inquiry remains | Final certainty, triumphant ending |

## Two sequences that must not be confused

### A. Playback order: S01 -> S02 -> ... -> S13

This is the full proposed story experience. No arbitrary shot is "chosen first" by the author. The sequence preserves the p.7-11 causal and inferential order.

### B. Production order: create shared references, then solve risky shots, then fill the spine

1. **Shot spine and provisional narration**: This document. Source fidelity is examined before image generation.
2. **Visual continuity pack**: Generate matching reference designs for one recurring person, notebook, partial room, engraved line-density/depth and black negative space. No user-supplied acting or camera footage.
3. **High-risk action anchors first**: Generate *matched start, intervention and end states* for **S08/S09 context/pressure** and **S11 reframed inquiry**. S04 making is next most demanding. The temporary source scene plate is only a style/space reference; it is not an approved master character.
4. **AI-first motion trials**: Animate one or more specific short actions using supported image-to-video/keyframe methods. Test the actual gesture and temporal identity. If only a six-frame GIF or camera pan results, report that as lower fidelity, **not successful native cinematic animation**.
5. **Build source-faithful less difficult shots**: S01/S02/S03, S05/S06/S07, S10/S12/S13 by deliberately authored image generation, animation, typography and editorial compositing. Only produce immersive room-to-world transformation if the shot's *meaning* requires it and the EX-001 correspondence contract can be satisfied.
6. **First integrated proof section**: Assemble **S08 -> S13** with imagery, genuine performance motion, provisional voice, sound and black holds. This directly tests the exact user-recognized strengths and failures before committing the whole 73-second film.
7. **Full 13-shot assembly**: Connect the introductory advice and accumulating world material, use exact voice/sound pacing and captions, and build the first complete illustrated vertical draft.
8. **A06 test + iteration**: Ask unfamiliar viewers what the advice, pressure and new inquiry meant before revealing intention. Separate problems attributable to source selection, narration, image, action, sound, model drift or transitions. Update force predictions prospectively, then improve only failed components.
9. **Final finish**: High-resolution frames, consistency/line survival, sound mix, captions, mobile checks and publication format.

**No invented model abilities:** This current chat can create still illustration assets and programmatic composited motion and prototype GIFs/MP4s. A dedicated image-to-video model's results must be separately generated and verified where tool access permits; not every animation method here can create native cinematic actor motion. Do not ask the user to film themselves as a workaround.

## Meaningful decision gates, with studio responsibility

- **G1 | Source and sequence:** Does the 13-shot spine preserve the essay's internal argument? *Studio derives; author can correct a substantive source misreading.*
