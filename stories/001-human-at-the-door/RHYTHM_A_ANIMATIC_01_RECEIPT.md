## Initial technical and creative inspection

**Established:** The mixed-mode sequence is concrete enough to view and time. It contains actual black holds, minimal constructions and one deliberately limited immersive event. A still-only black-and-white study is sufficient to begin evaluating film-level *editorial rhythm* without claiming that every frame is AI-generated video.

**Not established:**
1. **Comprehension:** No unprimed audience viewed it; do not infer any recognition result.
2. **Human pressure:** The current sketch may be too abstract before its immersive segment, and money/time/access stakes appear weakly in the frame language.
3. **Diagram semantics:** The placement arrow may falsely imply that a good answer **automatically** points to one predetermined real contact. Treat it as a test hypothesis, not final graphic.
4. **Motion proof:** The immersive passage cross-dissolves earlier EX-001 stills. It **does not fix** the known desk-to-table contour failure or validate AI temporal stability.
5. **Voice:** Speech is temporary synthetic narration, not final writing, recording or performance.
6. **Format:** 540 × 960 at 16fps is sufficient for a timing prototype, not a final distribution master.
7. **Purposeful black:** The blank holds may feel strong or like dead air; no actual viewer feedback yet decides.

- Does the opening establish a particular human's consequential question before abstraction takes over?
