# Artistic Constraint Contract v0.1

Status: proposed, not runtime-enforced. 2026-11-25.

Before producing a specimen or selecting a production method, recover its artistic question and protected meaning. Select relevant original KIRA forces and NOUSCERA cinematic forces, read the actual source, and derive a situated constraint, not a universal law. Use the existing [Scalpel Learning Loop](SCALPEL_LEARNING_LOOP_v01.md) for prediction and revision.

Required decision packet:

- `case_id`, protected meaning, author-approved style references and scope.
- `source_forces`: exact repository path, source version, force heading, mechanism and applicability limits; distinguish KIRA and NOUSCERA research.
- `constraints`: one observable artistic requirement, predicted failure, and comparison or observation that could disconfirm it for each selected force.
- `candidate_forms`: meaning-bearing alternatives and what remains fixed.
- `author_decision`: pending until explicit; distinguish artistic approval from technical success and viewer evidence.
- `method_handoff`: only after constraints and source identity are recorded, select appropriate native tool/method; record technical requirements separately.
- `receipt`: actual output identity, observations, misses, false vetoes, revision and transfer status.

Unknown or unsupported constraints stay candidate. Missing source blocks a source-grounded claim, not all exploratory work. No automatic approval or mutation. Do not encode this proposed contract as a hard runtime gate before testing it on artistic specimens.
