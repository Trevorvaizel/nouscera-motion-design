# Six artifacts: the research-to-film chain

**Approval boundary:** The author approved these six *artifact families*. Individual schemas are working proposals until used and reviewed.

## Two interdependent creative tracks

**A. Meaning:** original idea or authored source work -> **source map when adapting an existing work** -> selected protected claim -> intended recognition -> story alternatives -> viewer interpretation.  
**B. Cinematic language:** illustrated world -> spatial behavior -> motion grammar -> audiovisual atmosphere -> generation reliability.

The tracks meet in a **treatment-selection gate**. Visual quality cannot silently become the authority over meaning.

## Adaptation preflight (conditional, not a seventh artifact)

If the film draws from an existing long-form work, register the source, map its structure and tensions, and select an appropriate adaptation unit **before** the Thought Receipt. This prevents a quote or attractive metaphor from replacing the original narrative. Film 001 uses `stories/001-human-at-the-door/SOURCE_MAP_v01.md`. Do not place the full uploaded PDF into this public repository without a publication decision.

## Instruments and gates

| ID | Artifact | Must preserve / communicate | Admission gate | Destination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A01 | Thought Receipt | Exact authored thought, scope, tensions, qualifications, intended recognition, unapproved interpretations | Author confirms protected meaning | Treatment comparison |
| A02 | Treatment Comparison | At least two different visual metaphors, semantic transfer, risks, production uncertainty | One treatment explicitly selected, alternatives retained | Recognition score |
| A03 | Recognition Score | Beat-by-beat viewer inference **and speech-event-to-sign correspondence**: what is heard, what must become perceptible, visual scope and likely interpretation | Story logic works before expensive generation | Look / motion bible and shot design |
| A04 | Look and Motion Bible | Style constraints, line/black-field grammar, depth cues, transformation vocabulary, **isolated detailed objects, local scenes, black holds, independent scene changes**, test conditions | Stable enough to test, not yet universal law | Shot contracts |
| A05 | Shot and Transition Contracts | Starting state, changes, camera, timings, invariants, **required spatial continuity or meaningful cut**, failure cases, source assets and model settings | Reproducible shot experiment approved | Candidate takes and compositing |
| A06 | Selection and Evidence Receipt | Candidates, rejection reasons, defects, **what viewers inferred from heard-and-seen pairings**, what changes in method | Evidence supports scoped adoption or rework | Next creative cycle |

An approval is always **scoped**. Approving a thought does not approve a treatment; approving a look does not prove a working AI model; publishing a video does not validate the theory that inspired it.

In Film 001, [the speech-to-sign audit](../stories/001-human-at-the-door/A03_SPEECH_TO_SIGN_AUDIT_v01.md) maps the *actual temporary narration* to candidate visual referents and viewer inferences before another animatic revision. This remains an **A03 annex**, not a new seventh artifact.

## Feedback returns

A rejected take can force revision to A05 or A04 without rewriting A01. A contradictory viewer interpretation can send the work back to A02/A03, or reopen A01 only through the author. Record both the causal diagnosis and the scope of the revision.

**Principle:** each artifact is an instrument for the next creative decision, not a paperwork milestone.
