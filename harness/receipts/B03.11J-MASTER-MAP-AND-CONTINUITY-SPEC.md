## 5. How this is falsifiable

- **Trace test:** Can one locate the original essay page, force, shot role, current state and next operation in under a minute from the map? If not, simplify navigation.
- **Continuity test:** Do CP-01..CP-06 (once generated) show same character, notebook and coherent room while preserving meaningful two-context distinction?
- **Narrative test:** Would CP-03/04 imply “rich vs poor,” a fixed biography, or a moral winner instead of “same advice, different cost”? If so, revise before S08 motion.
- **Performance test:** Can actual S09 and S11 animation display **reaching/hesitating** and **a changed inquiry**? If not, do not claim scene success regardless of engraved beauty.
- **Version test:** Does each brick update the map and receipt while preserving original failed predictions and rejected assets?
