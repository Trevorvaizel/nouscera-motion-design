## One design question

How can The Transforming Room's black/white engraved imagery and fluid, spatial metamorphoses carry the essay's distinction between **potentially valid advice** and **advice placed into a person's actual situation**, without becoming empty spectacle?

## Inherited inputs

- _The Human at the Door_, "The fog", PDF pp. 7–11, held outside public GitHub.
- D-013: selected passage.
- D-014: approved meaning, placement rather than certainty with human pressures visible.
- D-015: the room **itself** becomes successive worlds.
- D-016: user's "proceed" authorizes A03 six-beat recognition structure as a working basis, not as proven viewer response.
- Engraved monochrome visual benchmark: black negative space, densely authored white contour and hatching, dimensional feel. Image content is not a mandate.
- P05/P06/P07/P08/P09/P10/P11/P12/P13: visual, animation, cinematic, AI and selection lenses, provisional.

## Output

[A04 Look and Motion Bible v0.1](../../stories/001-human-at-the-door/A04_LOOK_AND_MOTION_BIBLE_v01.md) defines:

1. Black as unformed space, not a villain; white etched strokes as world structure.
2. A visual hierarchy of primary subject, secondary spatial architecture and tertiary engraving.
3. Depth from contour, occlusion, coherence and perspective, not mandatory editable 3D.
4. Six semantic motion operations: emergence, formation, world conversion, interruption/hold, relational placement, selective retraction.
5. A mandatory semantic bridge across every world morph: source, target, preserved feature, implied relation, tether and likely false inference.
6. A provisional vertical/social test canvas, readability and flicker/accessibility checks.
7. The smallest meaningful proof: **one controlled room-to-advice-world transformation**, not a whole movie.

## Tradeoffs and countercases

- Detail and elaborate depth may overwhelm the legibility of the human question.
- A smooth transition may imply causal succession between advice options where none exists.
- A stable face or object is not synonymous with semantic continuity; a model can preserve a shape but lose the story.
- Restoring the room at the ending may falsely imply that no learning occurred.
- A visually successful morph is not evidence that viewers understand placement.
