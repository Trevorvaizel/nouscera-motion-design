# Open questions and uncertainty register

A question is closed only when an artifact, decision, or test resolves it; mark who closed it and point to the evidence. Do not invent an answer because a downstream file needs one.

| ID | Question | Blocking | Evidence or decision needed | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| OQ-002 | Which metaphor makes information-versus-orientation recognizable without importing a false single correct path? | Treatment selection | Compare at least two genuine visual alternatives | Derive Treatment Comparison after OQ-001 |
| OQ-003 | Which scene/transition best embodies appearance, metamorphosis, and absorption? | Look/motion bible | Visual experiments and continuity tests | Later stage |

| OQ-005 | What do first-time viewers actually infer when seeing the treatment? | Story approval | Cold-viewer recall and interpretation, not merely liking | After animatic / rough cut |
| OQ-006 | Which source claims in the 13 research manuscripts are directly verified versus secondary/abstract-level? | Promotion of evidence to canon | Targeted source audit | Ongoing research |
| OQ-007 | What is publishable in this public repository, including images, reference examples and private background documents? | Media migration | Provenance and rights review | Before adding binaries |
| OQ-008 | How will production takes and large media be stored without bloating Git history? | Asset workflow | Choose Git LFS or external storage and path manifest | Before large renders |

## Do not confuse uncertainties

An unresolved artistic choice is not the same as missing empirical evidence. A production risk is not the same as ambiguity in the author's claim. Keep each in the correct register.
