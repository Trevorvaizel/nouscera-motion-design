# Knowledge, approval and provenance policy

**Purpose:** Prevent the research library from becoming unchallengeable doctrine or the AI assistant's first draft from becoming the author's view.

## Status vocabulary

- **Source note:** Claims drawn from external material, tagged with precise references and limitations.
- **Candidate:** A research hypothesis, potential force, creative idea, or unexplored procedure.
- **Working:** A draft used for critique and comparison, not approval for release.
- **Approved for test:** Explicit author/creative-direction permission to try a defined version.
- **Tested:** Observation exists; report conditions, sample and defects.
- **Adopted:** Author accepts the rule or artifact within its demonstrated domain.
- **Revised / retired:** Replaced after contrary evidence or a better design; preserve the earlier rationale.

"13 provinces researched" means a systematic first-pass *desk library*, not total scholarly coverage. "109 candidate forces adjudicated" means a provisional classification, not confirmation by experimental evidence.

## Evidence hierarchy

Mark separately: (a) original or primary evidence, (b) credible secondary synthesis, (c) vendor/documentation claims, (d) theoretical interpretation, (e) the studio's own hypothesis, (f) creative taste or author direction, (g) results from a documented experiment. These categories are not interchangeable.

When using a source, identify **what exactly it supports**, not merely that it discusses the same subject. Keep disputed findings and countercases.

## Authority

- **Author:** Owns the central thought, intended meaning, creative approvals and publication.
- **Research/editorial:** May propose, compare, challenge and record uncertainty; cannot silently rewrite the author's claim.
- **AI generation:** Proposes images and motion; cannot grant itself artistic or evidentiary authority.
- **Viewer evidence:** Can contradict our expectation. Report what viewers actually reconstructed.
- **GitHub:** Captures history and collaboration; a commit or pull request is not itself proof of truth.

## Public repository boundary

This repository is **public**. Include only deliberate project-facing research and work. Do not upload the private KIRA foundation files, private conversation transcripts, user secrets, account info, copyrighted reference artworks without rights, generated image assets with unclear reuse terms, or raw exports with personal data. Use source references and explain methodological inheritance rather than duplicating private source documents.

Research publications must include source-register lineage and their working status. Avoid importing redundant superseded copies when a canonical working copy and Git history suffice.

## Change governance

Any material change to a story premise, chosen treatment, cinematic grammar, evidence claim, production gate or workflow requires:
1. The prior decision ID or prior artifact version.
2. The proposed difference.
3. Rationale, alternatives and countercase.
4. Explicit status and approver.
5. The downstream artifacts invalidated or needing review.
