## Method selection is an operational decision

[Registry](method-policy.v1.json) maps a structured objective and failure to a method, tool, evidence, status and required checks. Native [n8n decision workflow](method-decision.n8n.json), ID NousceraMethodDecision01, supplies a read-only decision packet. It does not approve, mutate, dispatch a render or verify claimed hashes on its own. ChatGPT remains coordinator: inspect the actual evidence, verify inputs and availability, invoke separately registered native operations, and retain receipts. No silent fallback to scripts when a native capability is missing. Code remains legitimate for kinematics, bounded source sampling, integration and verification where these are explicit method components. Prefer working in native applications instead of adding custom review UIs.

Retire the two rejected MAT webhooks (NousceraNativeMAT01 and NousceraMaterialMAT01) without deleting their definitions or executions. A new narrowly constrained texture hypothesis can use ComfyUI after explicit new registration and visual validation. Do not rerun broad or material-only rejected recipes as a production solution. Not a ban on MAT or generative methods generally.

## Creative run contract

1. Recover current shot intent, author decisions, source identity and applicable evidence. Verify original bytes and the approved manifest; no overwrites. Register a fresh experiment ID and version directory.
2. Classify objective/fault; invoke native method decision; unknown or rejected methods stop for coordinator review. For unfamiliar scene, request transfer_validation and register scene-specific evidence. Do not copy CP05 coordinates/weights blindly.
3. Define connected anatomy, rigid prop attachment, material ownership and occlusion. Use isolated cutouts, native rest/extreme pose tests, and real motion reveal sweep. Light and ink are separate. Keep head/support relationships coherent. Native application choice follows the operation, not convenience.
4. Check local anatomical shape and texture continuity, no detached tools/duplicated surfaces, and actual hidden reveals. Record observed failures. Do not infer readiness from exact pixels, alpha area, joint target error or successful node execution. If full readiness remains blocked, only an explicitly authorized isolated experimental preview may proceed, with blockers disclosed.
5. Render actual native frames and verify decoding and identity. Native Blender armature + analytic solve and native VSE are the demonstrated CP05 approach. The VSE foreground is baked from the rig scene, so rig edits require rerender. Preserve all external sequence dependencies. Publish a new Kitsu candidate with evidence, not approved status.
6. Show actual playback directly when possible (avoid unnecessary login). Author judgment applies to that exact revision. Record exact words, version, hashes, scope and remaining boundaries. Never silently promote method, preparation gate or film.
7. Update method registry only when evidence supports a changed disposition; preserve old versions/receipts. A repeated failure requires revisiting the method, not adding another concealment patch.

## Scope and remaining engineering

The decision workflow is a deterministic, structured selector, not an autonomous agent that understands arbitrary creative requests. It is not a universal interlock on all native tools. Coordinator classifies the problem, verifies actual inputs, and controls dispatch. Native n8n audit and publishing methods have their separate narrow contracts. New scene transfer, native live IK, complete clean-plate method, full head acting, generalized cross-app publication and off-device restore remain unproven.
