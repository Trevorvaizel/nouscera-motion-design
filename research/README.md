# Research library

**Status: Working research drafts, not approved cinematic laws.**

Read in order: [Volume 00](Volume_00_province_atlas.md), [Volume 14](Volume_14_integrative_synthesis_and_studio_constitution.md), [Stage 02](stage-02/STAGE_02_CRITICAL_SYNTHESIS_AND_DERIVATION.md), [force audit](stage-02/force_adjudication_ledger_109.md), then the individual [province manuscripts](provinces/). The [source register](SOURCE_REGISTER.csv) contains the first-pass bibliography.

The 13 provinces: P01 Cinematic Meaning; P02 Narrative and Rhetoric; P03 Multimedia Learning; P04 Attention and Perception; P05 Illustration and Aesthetics; P06 Cinematography; P07 Animation; P08 Motion Design and Metamorphosis; P09 Editing and Montage; P10 Sound and Voice; P11 Compositing and Effects; P12 Generative Video Systems; P13 Selection and Creative Judgment.

These drafts represent a systematic first-pass study across fields, not an exhaustive review of original evidence. Some supporting sources are abstracts, secondary accounts, publisher summaries or vendor references. The 109-force desk audit is a provisional classification, not an empirical validation. See [knowledge policy](../harness/KNOWLEDGE_POLICY.md).

The former full compendium and ZIP exports are intentionally not duplicated. Individual editable manuscripts plus Git history are the primary sources here. The KIRA source materials that inspired our *cartographic method* remain outside this public repo.
